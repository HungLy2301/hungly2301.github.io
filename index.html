About me

the short version

I grew up in Ho Chi Minh City and came to Ohio to study Financial Economics and Mathematics at Denison University. Along the way, my work has wandered through product management, strategy consulting, economic development, community marketing, education, and a small business of my own.

The common thread is that I like being the person who figures out what's really going on, then helps a team decide what to do about it. Sometimes that means a dashboard, sometimes a prototype, sometimes a good conversation with the people who'll use the thing.

These days I'm most curious about how AI and data can make products more useful, and how that work can open doors for communities like the one I come from.
